Wiring a Black Pill

WeAct STM32F411CE Black Pill wired for the tank build's full stack — LiPo into a power distribution board, PDB into both ESCs and the board's 5V rail, ESCs out to the brushless motors, and a CRSF receiver on the serial port. Pin names come straight from boards/blackpill_f411ce.h — this is a schematic wiring map, not the board's physical silkscreen layout, so match connections by label, not by position.

Battery sense divider — circuit

The component-level circuit behind the sense card above. A LiPo pack is far above the 3.3V the ADC can read, so a resistor divider scales it down. Only the three terminals are shown here — where they land on the board is in the wiring map above. 47k/5k6 reads a 4S at 1.79V and a 6S at 2.68V, both comfortably inside range.

Teal = the power chain: the LiPo feeds the PDB, the PDB feeds both ESCs and the board's 5V rail (which in turn powers the receiver). Navy = the three motor leads out of each ESC. Green = the ESC signal leads, the only motor wiring the board itself touches. Dashed violet = wired but not required today (CRSF telemetry back-channel, reserved for a future use of PA9). ESC 0 drives the left track and ESC 1 the right, both fed by the tank mixer rather than by a receiver channel directly.
