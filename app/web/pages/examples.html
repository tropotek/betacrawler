Wiring a Black Pill

WeAct STM32F411CE Black Pill wired for the tank build's dual-ESC / receiver stack. Pin names come straight from boards/blackpill_f411ce.h — this is a schematic wiring map, not the board's physical betacrawler layout, so match connections by label, not by position.

Dashed violet lead = wired but not required today (CRSF telemetry back-channel, reserved for a future use of PA9). Every other lead shown is load-bearing for the firmware as it stands. ESC 0 and ESC 1 are independently commanded today — no throttle/steering mixing yet.
